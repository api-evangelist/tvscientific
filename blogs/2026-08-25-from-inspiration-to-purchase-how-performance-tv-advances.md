In Pinterest’s latest earnings call, Bill Ready named “accelerating monetization through improved go-to-market and measurement capabilities” as one of the company’s three strategic priorities. Better monetization will only come from giving advertisers a clearer way to engage consumers across the full journey, measure what actually worked, and use those signals to improve performance over time. That is the opportunity we see with Performance TV, specifically as we approach it at tvScientific by Pinterest.
