For marketers, Labor Day has traditionally marked the unofficial end of summer. But it is increasingly useful to think of it as the beginning of the holiday shopping season. Consumers are already researching gifts before October, and the calendar quickly fills with moments that command their attention, from fall football to October Prime Day and eventually Black Friday Cyber Monday.
